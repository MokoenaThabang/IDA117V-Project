## What to record

- Who acquired the extract and checked the licence.
- Who audited the file and built the label.
- Who wrote the exploratory figures.
- Who fitted the baselines, the boosting model, and the neural networks.
- Who ran the search, the ablations, and McNemar's test.
- Who built the Streamlit form.
- Who drafted the report and who checked the numbers against `results/tables/`.
